Breakout pullback

A breakout is a close above the previous 20-day high on at least 1.5× the 20-day average volume, with Rs 1 Cr+ traded (one per stock per 10 sessions). Is the best time to buy the pullback after it, rather than the breakout day? Every entry is at the next open, with T+2 settlement (nothing sold before the 3rd session), 0.8% round-trip charges, bonus/right adjusted prices. The variants and exits were fixed before looking at the results.
