Before you buy anything

  1. Chart: confirm the base breakout, and that it isn't extended after a big run.
  2. Floorsheet: look for broad buying across several brokers, not one dominant broker.
  3. Trend: above the 50-day EMA and 200-day average, RSI not overbought.
  4. Fundamentals and news: no book closure, bad results or regulatory issue coming.
  5. Risk: set the stop first (suggested above), risk 1–2% of capital per trade.
