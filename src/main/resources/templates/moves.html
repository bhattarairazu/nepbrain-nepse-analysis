Move study

Which stock-days were followed by a tradeable move, and what did they have in common? A move means: buy at the next open, the high reaches the target within sessions (selling only from T+3), before a close below the entry. Liquid ordinary shares only (Rs 1 Cr+ traded that day), bonus/right adjusted prices. Averages are after 0.8% round-trip charges, before tax.
