How to read the lists

  • RVOL: today's volume ÷ the stock's average over the previous 20 sessions. Above 1.5× is unusual activity.
  • Close in range: where the close sits between the day's low and high. Green = closed in the top quarter (buyers held control), red = bottom quarter.
  • Avg trade: turnover ÷ number of transactions. Large and rising suggests bigger investors; one or two huge trades suggest a block deal.
  • Streak and last 10: how persistently the stock stays in the list. Persistence matters more than one day's rank.
  • new first appearance in 10 sessions. both also in the other list.
