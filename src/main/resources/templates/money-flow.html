What to look for

  • Many appearances and rising price: steady buying. These are the stocks to study on the chart.
  • Many appearances but falling price: heavy trading while sellers win, often distribution.
  • Few appearances, one high rank: a single big day, possibly a block deal. Check the stock page before reading anything into it.
  • Compare by turnover with by volume: a cheap stock can top the volume list with little money behind it.
