A rule can only be tested once its slowest indicator has enough history, so rules that need a 200-day average (3, 5, 9 and 1 with EMA 200) are tested on the last few months only. Each rule's window is split: the first 60% is the design period, the rest out-of-sample. A rule is marked profitable only with 30+ trades (10+ out-of-sample) and a positive average in both periods and a positive out-of-sample median. "NEPSE index" here is the app's equal-weight market of all ordinary shares ( over the stored period). Trades still open are not in the averages; they are shown under "Open now".
