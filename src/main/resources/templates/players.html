Big players

Footprints of large players ("operators") in two places. Price and volume: orders much bigger than the stock's normal trade size, turnover surges, near-circuit runs and how far the price has already gone. The floorsheet: a few brokers buying steadily, a top buyer that turned seller, or one broker on both sides of the trades.

Read this before using it. A footprint is not proof of who is trading or why. A broker number carries many clients, not one person; big tickets can be a mutual fund or a promoter; and the stocks operators push are the ones that later fall hardest, because they sell to late buyers. The useful part is the stage: entering during markup or pumped means buying from them.
