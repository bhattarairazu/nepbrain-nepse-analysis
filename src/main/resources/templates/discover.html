How it works

Every liquid stock-day (Rs 50 L+ average turnover) gets 28 indicator values, from data up to that day only:

The history is cut into three periods. Find (first half): a search over combinations of up to four conditions (each indicator above or below one of its quintile edges) keeps the ones whose trades did best. Choose (next quarter): the found rules are ranked on these sessions, which the search never saw. Check (last quarter): the final, untouched test. Trust a rule only if it is positive in the Check period too; searching many combinations always finds some that worked by luck.
