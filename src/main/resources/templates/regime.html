Check by hand: the money behind a bull run

NEPSE's big runs have been driven by liquidity. These are not in the app's data; check them in NRB's monetary policy and bank notices.

  • Interest rates falling: fixed-deposit and base rates lower than three months ago.
  • Banks have money to lend: credit-to-deposit ratio below its limit, interbank rate low.
  • NRB policy easing: margin-loan limits relaxed, policy rate cut, no new restrictions on share loans.
  • New investors: demat and Meroshare accounts growing, IPOs heavily oversubscribed.
